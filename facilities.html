Our Facilities

Home / Facilities

World-Class Infrastructure

Ram Krishna Vidya Mandir is equipped with state-of-the-art facilities designed to provide a holistic learning environment and nurture every dimension of student growth.

Technology

IT Education / Computer Lab

Our fully air-conditioned computer lab features modern workstations with high-speed broadband connectivity, empowering students with cutting-edge digital literacy from an early age. A structured coding curriculum prepares learners for the technology-driven future.

  • ✓ 60+ latest-generation computers with broadband internet
  • ✓ Structured coding curriculum (Scratch, Python, HTML)
  • ✓ Smart-board enabled classrooms for interactive learning
  • ✓ Certified faculty for ICT-based skill development
Innovation

Atal Tinkering Lab

Established under the NITI Aayog initiative, our Atal Tinkering Lab fosters a culture of scientific inquiry and innovation. Students engage hands-on with robotics, 3D printing, IoT devices, and electronics to build real-world solutions through STEM learning.

  • ✓ Robotics kits, Arduino boards & sensor modules
  • ✓ 3D printing & rapid prototyping equipment
  • ✓ IoT-based project development & coding workshops
  • ✓ National & regional science competition participation
Global Learning

International Headstart Program

Our International Headstart Program integrates a global curriculum framework alongside CBSE, exposing students to international best practices, cross-cultural awareness, and foreign language proficiency that prepares them for a globalised world.

  • ✓ Cambridge-aligned global curriculum integration
  • ✓ Foreign language exposure (French, Japanese basics)
  • ✓ International student exchange awareness programmes
  • ✓ Cross-cultural projects & Model United Nations
🧠
Wellness

Counselling Cell

Our dedicated Counselling Cell provides comprehensive support for student wellness, career guidance, and mental health. Trained professional counsellors offer one-on-one sessions, group workshops, and parent interactions to ensure each child thrives emotionally and academically.

  • ✓ Certified child psychologist on campus
  • ✓ Personalised career guidance & aptitude assessments
  • ✓ Mental health awareness sessions & stress management
  • ✓ Parent-counsellor interaction programmes
🎓
Enrichment

Workshops & Seminars

Regular workshops, seminars, and guest lectures by industry experts, academicians, and alumni enrich our students beyond the textbook. These sessions spark curiosity, build 21st-century skills, and expose learners to diverse career paths and emerging fields.

  • ✓ Monthly expert-led guest lectures & panel discussions
  • ✓ Hands-on skill development workshops (art, tech, finance)
  • ✓ Alumni mentorship & career networking events
  • ✓ Inter-school symposiums & debate competitions
🌐
Communication

Language Lab

Our state-of-the-art Language Lab is designed to enhance multi-lingual proficiency and communication skills through audio-visual aids, interactive software, and structured practice sessions. Students build confidence in spoken English, Hindi, and introductory foreign languages.

  • ✓ Audio-visual learning aids & headset stations
  • ✓ Multi-lingual training (English, Hindi, Sanskrit)
  • ✓ Interactive pronunciation & grammar software
  • ✓ Public speaking & elocution practice sessions
Knowledge Hub

Library

The RKVM Central Library houses an extensive collection of over 10,000 books, periodicals, journals, and digital resources. It serves as a vibrant knowledge hub with dedicated reading zones, research corners, and structured reading programmes for all grades.

  • ✓ 10,000+ books, encyclopaedias & reference materials
  • ✓ Digital resources, e-books & online journal access
  • ✓ Dedicated reading programmes & book clubs
  • ✓ Quiet study zones & group discussion areas

Experience Our Campus First-Hand

Words and pictures can only convey so much. We invite you to visit Ram Krishna Vidya Mandir, walk through our classrooms, labs, and grounds, and feel the vibrant energy of our learning community for yourself.
