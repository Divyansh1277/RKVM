Academic Programmes

Our rigorous CBSE curriculum is delivered through innovative teaching methodologies that ensure deep conceptual understanding. We offer comprehensive streams in Science, Commerce, and Arts at the senior secondary level.

Every classroom is equipped with smart boards and digital learning tools. Our experienced faculty of over 150 teachers brings passion and expertise, ensuring personalized attention for every student.

✓ CBSE Curriculum — Classes I to XII
✓ Science, Commerce, and Arts Streams
✓ Smart Classrooms with Digital Learning
✓ Experienced & Qualified Faculty
✓ Regular Assessments & Progress Reports

Beyond Academics

We believe in developing the whole child through a rich tapestry of extracurricular activities.

🏏

Sports

Cricket, football, athletics, badminton, volleyball, and more. Our students compete at district and state levels, with dedicated coaches and modern sports infrastructure.

🎵

Music & Dance

Classical and contemporary music and dance training fosters creativity and cultural appreciation. Regular performances at school events and inter-school competitions.

🎨

Art & Craft

Our art studio provides a creative sanctuary for students to explore painting, sculpture, pottery, and mixed media. Regular art exhibitions showcase student talent.

🎭

Drama & Theatre

Theatre arts develop confidence, communication, and empathy. Students perform in annual productions and participate in inter-school drama competitions.

💬

Debate & Quiz

Sharpening analytical thinking and public speaking skills through debate clubs, Model United Nations, quiz bowls, and elocution competitions.

🤝

Community Service

Social responsibility is integral to our curriculum. Students engage in village outreach, environmental drives, charity events, and mentorship programmes.

Centre of Excellence

Ram Krishna Vidya Mandir has established strategic collaborations with leading universities and institutions to provide students with exposure beyond the regular curriculum. Our Centre of Excellence programme prepares students for national and international competitions.

Through dedicated training programmes, our students excel in Olympiads, NTSE, KVPY, and other competitive examinations, gaining a significant edge in their academic journey.

✓ University collaborations and guest lectures
✓ Science & Math Olympiad training
✓ NTSE & KVPY preparation
✓ Student research projects and presentations
