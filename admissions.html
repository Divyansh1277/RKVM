Admission Procedure

Follow these simple steps to secure admission at Ram Krishna Vidya Mandir. Our streamlined process ensures a smooth experience for every family.

Step 1 — Submit Enquiry

Fill out the Admission Query form or visit the school office to express your interest. Our team will guide you through the requirements and share all necessary information about the school, curriculum and facilities.

Step 2 — Document Verification

Submit the required documents including previous mark-sheets, transfer certificate, Aadhaar card, birth certificate, passport-size photographs and caste certificate (if applicable). Our office will verify all documents within 2–3 working days.

Step 3 — Entrance Assessment

Students will appear for an age-appropriate entrance assessment in English, Mathematics and General Knowledge. The test evaluates the student's readiness for the class applied for and is designed to be stress-free.

Step 4 — Interview

A brief interaction session is conducted with the student and parents. The interview helps us understand the child's interests, aptitude and the family's expectations from the school.

Step 5 — Fee Payment & Enrollment

Upon selection, parents complete the admission formalities by paying the applicable fees and signing the enrollment agreement. A welcome kit with school uniform details, book list and academic calendar is provided on the spot.

Fee Structure

Transparent and affordable fee structure for all classes. Fees are payable quarterly. Sibling discount and scholarship concessions available upon eligibility.

info Important Notes

  • All fees are quoted per annum and are subject to revision.
  • A one-time admission fee of ₹5,000 is applicable for new students.
  • Hostel charges are separate — please refer to the Hostel page for details.
  • Sibling discount of 10% on tuition fee is available for the second child onwards.
  • Scholarships are offered to meritorious students based on entrance assessment performance.
