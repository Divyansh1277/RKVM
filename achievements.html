Awards & Recognition

Celebrating our school's achievements in academics, sports, and cultural activities.

🔬

National Science Olympiad

Multiple students qualified for national rounds, with 3 gold medals and 5 silver medals in the past 3 years at the National Science Olympiad competition.

🏆

State Sports Championship

Winners of Bihar State Inter-School Cricket Championship 2024 and runners-up in Football. Multiple athletes selected for state-level competitions.

🧠

CBSE Heritage Quiz

Our quiz team reached the zonal finals of the CBSE Heritage India Quiz in 2024, demonstrating exceptional knowledge of Indian culture and heritage.

🎭

Cultural Fest Winners

First prize in Inter-School Cultural Festival 2024 for group dance, solo singing, and one-act play categories across Bihar region.

🏅

Best School Award

Recognized as the "Best School in Academic Excellence" by the Bihar Education Board for three consecutive years (2022, 2023, 2024).

🌿

Green School Certification

Awarded Green School Certification for outstanding environmental practices, including solar panels, rainwater harvesting, and campus greening initiatives.
