Welcome to Ram Krishna Vidya Mandir

Founded in 1995, Ram Krishna Vidya Mandir stands as one of the most esteemed educational institutions in Bhagalpur, Bihar. Affiliated with the Central Board of Secondary Education (CBSE), our school is committed to providing a world-class education that combines academic excellence with moral and ethical values.

Located at Gandhi Nikunj, Rannuchak, Bhagalpur, our sprawling campus provides a stimulating environment where students discover their potential and develop into confident, responsible citizens. With state-of-the-art facilities, dedicated faculty, and a curriculum that goes beyond textbooks, we prepare our students for the challenges of tomorrow.

At RKVM, we believe that every child is unique and deserving of an education that nurtures their individual talents. Our holistic approach integrates academics, sports, arts, and community service to shape well-rounded individuals ready to make a positive impact on society.

Our Educational Philosophy

At Ram Krishna Vidya Mandir, we are guided by the timeless principles of holistic education. We believe that true learning extends far beyond the classroom — it encompasses character building, ethical values, social responsibility, and creative expression.

Our curriculum is designed to develop the intellectual, physical, emotional, and spiritual dimensions of each student. We emphasize critical thinking, collaborative learning, and real-world problem-solving skills that prepare our students for global challenges.
