Hostel Facilities

Home/Hostel

A Home Away From Home

Our hostel provides a comfortable, safe, and nurturing environment for students from outside Bhagalpur. With 24/7 warden supervision, nutritious meals, and dedicated study hours, we ensure that hostel students receive the same care and attention as they would at home.

The hostel accommodates both boys and girls in separate wings, with modern amenities that make student life comfortable and productive. Our focus is on creating a disciplined yet friendly atmosphere that encourages academic growth and personal development.

Boys & Girls Wings 24/7 Security Nutritious Meals CCTV Monitored

Hostel Amenities

Everything your child needs for a comfortable and productive stay.

🛏️

Spacious Rooms

Well-ventilated rooms with comfortable beds, personal study desks, and adequate storage space for personal belongings.

🍽️

Nutritious Dining

Hygienic kitchen serving balanced vegetarian meals — breakfast, lunch, evening snacks, and dinner prepared under supervision.

📚

Study Hall

Dedicated study rooms with proper lighting and quiet environment for focused preparation during designated study hours.

🎮

Recreation

Common room with indoor games, TV, and a small library for leisure time. Outdoor sports facilities also accessible.

👔

Laundry

Regular laundry service ensures students always have clean uniforms and personal clothing without any hassle.

🏥

Medical Care

On-campus medical room with first-aid facilities. Regular health check-ups and tie-up with nearby hospital for emergencies.

📶

Wi-Fi Access

Supervised internet access for academic research and online learning during designated hours.

🔒

24/7 Security

Round-the-clock security with CCTV surveillance, biometric attendance, and controlled entry/exit for student safety.

Rules & Regulations

  • Students must adhere to the daily schedule including wake-up, study, meals, and lights-out times
  • Mobile phones and electronic gadgets are not permitted without special permission
  • Students must maintain cleanliness and hygiene in their rooms and common areas
  • Ragging in any form is strictly prohibited and will result in immediate expulsion
  • Students must obtain written permission from the warden before leaving the hostel premises
  • Visitors are allowed only during designated visiting hours on weekends
  • Damage to hostel property will be charged to the student's account
  • All students must participate in hostel study hours from 7:00 PM to 9:30 PM
