🎯 Our Mission

To provide a nurturing and stimulating educational environment that empowers every student to achieve academic excellence while developing strong moral character, critical thinking skills, and a deep sense of social responsibility. We strive to create holistic individuals who are prepared to thrive in an ever-changing world.

🔭 Our Vision

To be recognized as a leading educational institution that sets the benchmark for quality education in Bihar and beyond. We envision a school where innovation meets tradition, where every student discovers their unique potential, and where education serves as the catalyst for positive transformation in society.

Our Founding Story

Established in 1995 by a group of visionary educationists, Ram Krishna Vidya Mandir was born from the dream of providing world-class education to the children of Bhagalpur and surrounding regions. Inspired by the teachings of Swami Vivekananda, the founders believed that education is the most powerful tool for social transformation.

Our Journey

Key milestones in the growth and evolution of Ram Krishna Vidya Mandir.

1995 — School Founded

Ram Krishna Vidya Mandir was established at Gandhi Nikunj, Rannuchak, Bhagalpur with a vision to provide quality education rooted in Indian values. Started with 120 students and 12 teachers.

2003 — CBSE Affiliation

The school achieved CBSE affiliation, a landmark moment that recognized our academic standards and commitment to quality education at the national level.

2010 — New Campus Expansion

A major campus expansion added modern science labs, a computer center, sports facilities, and a new academic wing, accommodating over 1500 students.

2018 — Atal Tinkering Lab

Selected by NITI Aayog to establish an Atal Tinkering Lab, bringing STEM innovation, robotics, and 3D printing to our students for hands-on learning.

2024 — Digital Campus Initiative

Launched the Digital Campus initiative with smart classrooms, an online learning management system, and high-speed internet across the campus for 21st-century learning.
